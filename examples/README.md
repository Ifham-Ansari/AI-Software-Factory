## Writing your own brief

A good brief makes the PM's questions short and the plan accurate:

* **Say who uses it and why** ("customers book from their phone so we stop taking calls").
* **Describe the main flows** step by step, in plain words.
* **Name the rules** that matter (who can see what, what makes a slot free, what happens on cancel).
* **List what is out of scope.** Agents build only what you ask for, and this keeps it that way.
* **Pick a stakes level** (Hobby project, Internal tool, Customer-facing, or Regulated). It sets how much polish, testing, and security review the team does.
* **Attach screenshots or links** of apps you like. The designer uses them for the visual directions.

For a small change to a project that already exists, one sentence is enough ("make the header sticky on mobile"). Leave the process on **Auto** and it goes straight to an engineer.
