# Example idea: booking app for a barbershop

A medium-sized brief that runs the **full** track: questions, PRD, design directions, architecture, and a parallel build. Paste everything below the line into the Idea screen.

---

I run a small barbershop with three barbers. I want a booking website so customers can stop calling us.

Customers pick a service (haircut, beard trim, or both), then a barber or "anyone", then a free time slot in the next two weeks. They enter their name, phone number, and email, and get a confirmation email with a link to cancel. They should not need an account.

Each barber has working hours per weekday and can block off time (lunch, holidays). A slot is only free if the chosen barber is free for the whole length of the service.

I need a simple admin page, protected by a password, where I see today's and tomorrow's bookings per barber, can cancel a booking, and can edit barbers' hours.

It should look clean and modern, work well on a phone (most customers book from their phone), and show our opening hours and address on the home page.

Out of scope for now: online payments, SMS reminders, reviews, and a mobile app.

**Stakes:** customer-facing
